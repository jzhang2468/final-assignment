Spatial Canvases / p5.js + Three.js

Abstract Architecture Archive

Four spatial studies imagine an archive as a navigable architectural system: interior corridors, atriums, vaults, structural frames, material shelves, moving index marks, and drawings that behave like living plans.

3D-01

Archive Atrium

Travel inside a long archive atrium with walls, beams, columns, mezzanines, stairs, shelves, light wells, and a moving camera path.

3D-02

Material Vault

Move through a foggy material vault lined with ribs, display cases, shelves, section planes, fragments, and shifting inspection light.

2D-01

Plan Catalogue

A static p5.js floor plan organizes rooms around a central atrium with corridors, doors, stairs, section marks, labels, north arrow, and scale.

2D-02

Index Drift

An animated p5.js catalogue field lets the cursor act as an archival lens, pulling labels, dimensions, and drawing fragments into focus.

Design Interest

The theme is an interactive abstract architecture archive. I am using code to treat architectural records as inhabitable material: plans have circulation, fragments become rooms, labels become wayfinding, and the archive becomes a building you can move through.
